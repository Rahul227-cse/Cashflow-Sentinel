# CashFlow Sentinel — 9-slide pitch

1. **Title:** Explainable cash-flow intelligence for Indian MSME suppliers.
2. **Problem:** Late and inconsistent B2B payments force manual reconciliation and uncertain collections.
3. **User:** Small suppliers managing invoices, partial payments and remittance references.
4. **Solution:** A dashboard that ranks collection work and highlights evidence-backed suspected discrepancies.
5. **How it works:** Exasol stores synthetic operational data; Flask exposes analytical results; React presents action queues.
6. **Explainability:** Heuristic risk score shows history, timing, balance and payment behaviour contributions.
7. **Reconciliation:** Duplicate invoices, partial/mismatched payments, unmatched payments and deduction references are flagged for human review.
8. **Differentiation:** Local, reproducible, SQL-backed workflow with traceable record IDs—not black-box fraud claims.
9. **Impact hypothesis:** Faster prioritization and investigation for suppliers; requires real-world validation before claiming savings.
